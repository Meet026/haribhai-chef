# Hero Section Requirement Plan: Cinematic Entrance

## 1. The Vision
The Hero section is designed to be a "Cinematic Entrance" for **Haribhai Rasoiya**. It should feel like a luxury opening of a high-end film or an editorial magazine, moving from a focused, intimate view to a grand, immersive experience.

---

## 2. Phase 1: The Grand Reveal (First Impression)
- **The Curtain**: When the site first loads, a warm tan "curtain" slides away elegantly, revealing the content behind it.
- **The Oval Portal**: The main brand image first appears inside a sophisticated vertical oval frame, creating a sense of focus and boutique quality.

---

## 3. Phase 2: The Living Experience (Idle State)
- **Gentle Breathing**: While the user is just looking at the page without scrolling, the central image and the brand name have a "breathing" effect—slowly and subtly floating up and down to make the site feel alive and premium.
- **Ambient Glow**: A soft, warm golden glow exists in the background, paired with tiny floating particles that mimic dust motes in sunlight.

---

## 4. Phase 3: The Scrolling Journey (The Transition)
As the user begins to scroll down, the interface responds with a synchronized transformation:
- **Vanishing Branding**: The large brand name and the curved tagline gracefully fade and move out of the way to let the imagery take center stage.
- **Morphing Frame**: The small oval frame gradually expands. It starts as a narrow oval, grows into a rounded rectangle, and finally stretches to cover the entire screen.
- **Immersive Zoom**: As the frame expands, the image inside slowly zooms in, creating a "pull-through" effect that draws the visitor deeper into the brand's world.

---

## 5. Visual Standards
- **Typography**: The brand name uses a high-contrast, elegant serif font (italicized) to communicate tradition and luxury.
- **The Tagline**: A secondary tagline is displayed on a gentle curve at the bottom of the portal, adding to the boutique editorial feel.
- **Color Palette**: The environment is dark and moody (Charcoal) to make the warm food imagery and golden accents truly pop.

---

## 6. Experience Goals
- **Smoothness**: Every movement must be fluid and "heavy"—feeling more like luxury clockwork than a quick digital snap.
- **Responsive Adaptation**: Whether on a large desktop or a phone, the oval expansion must feel perfectly centered and intentional.
